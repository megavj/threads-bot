188 Threads Bot – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-21 · Operator: 188 Division, Prime Asset (김상현) · Contact: ksh1629@gmail.com

1. What this app does

188 Threads Bot is an internal tool used only by the operator to manage the operator's own Threads account (@iprimeasset). It drafts posts, publishes posts the operator has approved, shows replies and mentions so the operator can respond, searches public posts on insurance-industry topics so the operator can join relevant conversations, and shows post insights.

2. Meta / Threads data we access

  • Threads user ID and username of the connected account (threads_basic)
  • Posts the operator publishes through the app (threads_content_publish)
  • Replies to the operator's posts and replies the operator writes (threads_read_replies, threads_manage_replies)
  • Posts that mention the operator's account (threads_manage_mentions)
  • Public posts returned by keyword search: post ID, text, username, link, time (threads_keyword_search)
  • Insights for the operator's posts and profile: views, likes, replies, reposts, followers (threads_manage_insights)
  • An access token issued by Meta

3. Why we use it

Only to publish and manage the operator's own content and to respond to conversations. We do not sell, rent, or share Threads data, do not use it for advertising, do not build profiles of other users, and do not access direct messages.

4. Storage and retention

The access token is stored encrypted in the macOS Keychain on the operator's computer. Post IDs, links and reply IDs are kept locally to avoid duplicate posting and duplicate notifications. Public search results are used for the day's work and deleted within 30 days. Insights are kept as aggregate numbers only.

5. Deleting your data

You can remove the app at any time in Threads: Settings → Account → Website permissions → Remove "188 Threads Bot". To request deletion of any data relating to you, email ksh1629@gmail.com with your Threads username; we delete it within 30 days and reply with a confirmation. Details: Data deletion instructions.
